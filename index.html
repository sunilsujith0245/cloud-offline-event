Coach Karthikeyan Selvaraj · Founder, Pro Foodpreneur School

Coach Karthikeyan didn't start in food. He was an engineer at General Motors, with a stable job and a predictable future ahead of him. Then he walked away from it — for a 60 sq ft kitchen and a rent of just ₹5,000 a month.

That small kitchen grew into one of India's most respected food business mentorship ecosystems, built on what actually works in the real world, not theory borrowed from a classroom.

Every framework he teaches has been tested where it matters: in home kitchens, cloud kitchens, QSRs, cafés and fine dining, across India and beyond.

What exactly is the Pro Foodpreneur Intensive?

A one-day, in-person bootcamp on Foundation, Branding and Execution. You work on your own business through the day instead of just listening.

Where exactly is the venue?

The Intensive is near Bannerghatta Road, Bangalore. The exact address is shared after you register.

What's included in the price?

A certificate, a bonus pack (templates, layouts, checklists and AI prompts), and all meals: two tea breaks and lunch.

I've never run a food business before. Is this still for me?

Yes. It's built for people starting out and people already running a business. The Foundation part brings everyone up to speed before the exercises begin.

I'm already running a food business. Is this still helpful for me?

Yes. The Execution part — 1 Crore Revenue Roadmap, Hiring & Managing Your Kitchen Team, Unit Economics — is most useful if you're already running a business: fixing margins, building a team that doesn't depend on you, and planning growth.

Do I need to bring anything?

Just yourself, and a notebook if you'd like one. All other materials are provided on the day.
