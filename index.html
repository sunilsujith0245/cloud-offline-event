What exactly is the Pro Foodpreneur Intensive?

A one-day, in-person bootcamp on Foundation, Branding and Execution. You work on your own business through the day instead of just listening.

Where exactly is the venue?

The Intensive is at The Park, Hyderabad. View on Google Maps ↗ Venue is subject to change.

What's included in the price?

A certificate, a bonus pack (templates, layouts, checklists and AI prompts), and all meals: two tea breaks and lunch.

I've never run a food business before. Is this still for me?

Yes. It's built for people starting out and people already running a business. The Foundation part brings everyone up to speed before the exercises begin.

I'm already running a food business. Is this still helpful for me?

Yes. The Execution part (1 Crore Revenue Roadmap, Hiring & Managing Your Kitchen Team, Unit Economics) is mostly useful if you're already running a business: fixing margins, building a team that doesn't depend on you, and planning growth.

Do I need to bring anything?

Just yourself and a notebook if you'd like one. All other materials are provided on the day.
